# BizBridge demo storyboard (80 seconds)

## Recording plan

Capture a single 80-second walkthrough using a clearly labelled demo account
and synthetic transactions. Do not show real customer details, payment
references, or credentials. Use only data that is visibly identified as demo
data.

| Time | Screen / action | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8s | Title card: BizBridge and the youth entrepreneurship problem. | “Many small businesses trade every day but still struggle to organise records and prepare for formal opportunities.” |
| 8–18s | Open the dashboard and show the demo business profile. | “BizBridge brings the basics together: a business profile and a simple record of income and expenses.” |
| 18–30s | Point to monthly income, expenses, net cash flow, and transaction count. | “Owners can see what they have recorded this month, without treating incomplete records as the whole picture.” |
| 30–42s | Show the six-month cash-flow trend and spending categories. Point to the basis statement and a historical warning. | “The insight view groups recorded transactions by month and expense category. Warnings explain the period and are historical observations, not predictions.” |
| 42–59s | Open the Readiness Passport. Toggle a self-reported item and show the percentage and next steps update. | “The passport combines checks based on BizBridge records with clearly labelled self-reported preparation steps. Progress is saved to the business account.” |
| 59–70s | Show the payment alert and opportunity screens, if available in the demo environment. | “Review alerts prompt a closer look; they do not claim fraud. Opportunity discovery points owners toward possible formal-market routes.” |
| 70–80s | End card with product name and next step. | “BizBridge helps founders move from activity to clearer records and practical next steps. We’re looking for founders and support organisations to help validate what matters most.” |

## On-screen safeguards

- Label demo data as synthetic.
- Do not imply that checklist completion guarantees funding, registration, or
  procurement eligibility.
- Do not claim that a duplicate reference proves fraud.
- Keep the financial insights basis visible when showing the trend.
