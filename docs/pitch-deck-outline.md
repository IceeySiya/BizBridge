# BizBridge hackathon pitch deck

The companion PDF contains eight slides. Keep the live pitch to roughly three
minutes and use the demo storyboard for the separate 80-second product video.

1. **BizBridge — from trading to readiness**  
   Youth entrepreneurship and small-business growth; focus on township and
   rural founders.
2. **The gap**  
   Daily business activity can leave fragmented records, limited cash-flow
   visibility, and difficult preparation for formal markets.
3. **The idea**  
   A lightweight business operating workflow: start, record, understand, and
   prepare.
4. **Product walkthrough**  
   Profile, transaction entry, monthly overview, six-month insights, readiness
   passport, and opportunity discovery.
5. **What is different**  
   One joined-up path from records to next steps; explicit distinction between
   recorded evidence and self-reported preparation.
6. **Responsible by design**  
   Owner-scoped data, authentication, historical-only financial insight
   warnings, and no implied lending or fraud guarantees.
7. **Validation plan**  
   Test with founders and support practitioners; measure activation, repeat
   bookkeeping, checklist usefulness, and opportunity follow-through.
8. **The ask**  
   Invite founders, business-support organisations, and market-access partners
   to test the MVP and help validate the next priorities.

No market-size, traction, revenue, partnership, or impact figures are claimed
in this draft because the project has not supplied validated evidence for
those claims.
